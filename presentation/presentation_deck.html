The index appreciated from ~14,551 (Sept 2021) to ~23,450 (Aug 2026). The 50-day and 200-day Simple Moving Averages acted as primary structural dynamic boundaries.

Daily returns exhibit negative skewness and excess kurtosis (> 3.0), verifying that severe downward moves happen far more frequently than predicted by a normal distribution.

Large price changes tend to be followed by large changes, of either sign. Rolling 20-day annualized volatility varied from 9.5% during steady bull phases to 27.4% during macroeconomic corrections.

Econometric Finding: The Naive Persistence model achieves the lowest level-price RMSE. In financial economics, stock prices approximate a martingale random walk ($\mathbb{E}[P_{t+1} \mid \mathcal{F}_t] \approx P_t$), making today's price the minimum-variance quadratic estimator of tomorrow's price level. Directional accuracy for the naive model is reported as N/A as it predicts zero price movement.

8. Statistical Significance Analysis & Scientific Honesty

Slide 8 of 10

Formal Hypothesis Testing: We conducted an exact one-sided Binomial test against the random coin-toss null hypothesis ($H_0: p = 0.50$):

  • XGBoost Directional Accuracy: 108 / 208 days (51.92%), $p = 0.3138$ (Fail to reject $H_0$).
  • LSTM Directional Accuracy: 114 / 208 days (54.81%), $p = 0.0938$ (Moderate trend, not statistically significant at 5%).
Scientific Integrity: We do NOT claim statistically significant outperformance. Neither ML nor Deep Learning statistically beats a 50% random walk on out-of-sample daily equity index closes, confirming semi-strong market efficiency.

9. Future Target Price Forecasting Horizon (FR6)

Slide 9 of 10

Real Predictive Modeling (No Fixed Drift): Projections are driven by true recursive model rollouts:

Statistical ARIMA:

Uses get_forecast(steps=H) from statsmodels, computing expected paths and parametric confidence intervals based on the estimated covariance matrix.

Recursive Tree Ensembles:

Forecasts step $t+1$, appends it to price history, dynamically recalculates all 15+ indicators (SMA, RSI, MACD, Volatility), and predicts $t+2$ up to $t+H$ with compounding test error corridors ($z \cdot RMSE \cdot \sqrt{h}$).

Customizable in App: Users can select horizons ($T+1$ to $T+30$ trading days) and confidence bands (80% to 99%).
